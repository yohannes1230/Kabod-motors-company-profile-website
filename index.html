Corporate Overview

Built on Quality.
Positioned for Growth.

Established in 2016 in Addis Ababa by Abiy Belay Belete, Kabod Motors was created with a clear directive: to bridge the Ethiopian market with dependable, internationally certified automotive, energy, and heavy machinery products.
